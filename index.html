Meet the Coast Charger.

The Coast Charger is a solar-powered charging station for e-bikes. It's portable and fully off-grid, so you can set it up at a coffee shop, farmer's market, or the beach! Each station can charge 5 e-bikes at once, so feel free to ride around town with the whole family!
